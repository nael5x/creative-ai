# Code of Conduct

Creative AI aims to keep contribution discussions respectful, technical, and focused on improving the project.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- give actionable technical feedback;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, spam, or discriminatory behavior;
- respect maintainer decisions about scope and verification;
- avoid publishing private or sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior may result in comments, issues, or contributions being moderated or rejected.

For now, moderation is handled by the primary maintainer, [@nael5x](https://github.com/nael5x).
