HOW THIS WORKS

Trust before scale.

Creative AI keeps the full maintained directory available for discovery, but a reviewed badge is earned only after checking an official source and confirming concrete user value. Legacy entries that have not been re-reviewed are labeled pending audit rather than being presented as verified. Tools that fail the policy are corrected or removed instead of being kept to preserve a large catalog number.
