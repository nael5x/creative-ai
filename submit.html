How review works

We verify the official identity, confirm AI is a real first-class capability, check that the product is currently usable, and look for clear practical value. Third-party directories can help us discover a tool, but they are never enough evidence by themselves.

Thin wrappers, dead products, duplicate aliases, spam/SEO shells and unsupported marketing claims are rejected even if that makes the catalog smaller.
